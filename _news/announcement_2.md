Our paper on exercise assessment with wearable sensors is out in *Frontiers in Medical Technology*. ADA (anthropometry and diagnostic aware deep learning) classifies squat and Romanian deadlift repetitions as correct or incorrect from 17 Xsens IMUs, and adds each person's body measurements and diagnostic profile to the model.

Fifteen healthy adults each performed 10 correct and 10 incorrect repetitions of both exercises. With subject-wise validation, accuracy rose from 86.5% with the kinematics alone to 94.8% with the personal features, and movement-related risk was predicted with 97.8% accuracy.

- Paper: [Frontiers in Medical Technology](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmedt.2025.1725661)
