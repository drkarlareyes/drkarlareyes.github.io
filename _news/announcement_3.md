I'm excited to share the release of our **Lumbar Spine X-ray Dataset**, an open resource designed to support research at the intersection of **biomechanics, medical imaging, and artificial intelligence**.

The dataset contains **714 lateral lumbar spine X-ray images**, including **698 manually annotated images** with vertebral bounding boxes and anatomical landmarks (L1–L5 and S1). These annotations enable the development of computer vision algorithms for automated spinal analysis, vertebral localization, and spondylolisthesis assessment.

The dataset includes:

- 🦴 714 sagittal lumbar spine X-ray images
- 📍 698 images with vertebral bounding boxes and anatomical keypoints
- 🤖 Annotations compatible with PyTorch Keypoint R-CNN
- 📐 Standardized image preprocessing for reproducible AI research

This resource was developed to facilitate research in **spine biomechanics**, **computer vision**, and **AI-assisted musculoskeletal diagnosis**. Beyond automated grading of spondylolisthesis, we hope it will support future work in vertebral morphology analysis, posture assessment, landmark detection, and clinical decision support systems.

The dataset is openly available for the research community under a **CC BY 4.0** license.

💻 **Dataset:** https://github.com/drkarlareyes/vertebrae-dataset
