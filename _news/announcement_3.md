Our Spondylolisthesis Vertebral Landmark dataset is open on Mendeley Data (CC BY 4.0). It holds 716 lateral lumbar spine X-rays: 208 from Honduran patients diagnosed with spondylolisthesis and 508 from the public BUU-LSPINE dataset. In 698 of them, each vertebra from L3 to S1 is annotated with a bounding box and its four corners, in a JSON format that Keypoint R-CNN reads directly.

- Data: [Mendeley Data](https://doi.org/10.17632/5jdfdgp762.1)
- Code: [spondylolisthesis-detection](https://github.com/drkarlareyes/spondylolisthesis-detection)
