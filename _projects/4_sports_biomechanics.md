I study how athletes and trainees move, with two aims: assessing technique automatically, and making biomechanical analysis affordable where specialised laboratories are not available.

#### Publications

- Reyes Leiva KM, Nikelova P, Cerny M. Anthropometry and diagnostic aware deep learning for exercise assessment. *Frontiers in Medical Technology* 7:1725661 (2026). Squat and Romanian deadlift quality from 17 Xsens IMUs plus anthropometric and diagnostic features, with attention-based fusion and personal fine-tuning: 94.8% accuracy with subject-wise validation, against 86.5% for kinematics alone. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmedt.2025.1725661)
- Diaz C, Diaz H, Reyes K. Decoding the soccer pass, a kinematic analysis using Kinovea and inertial sensors. *Applied Mechanics and Materials* 929:119-128 (2025). A low-cost system to evaluate pass quality; speed was related to flexion of the supporting knee. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.4028/p-TNzjS4)
- Guevara García TC, Berlioz Parada MF, Reyes Leiva KM. Biomechanical analysis of functional movement in athletes using Kinovea. *Advances in Digital Health and Medical Bioengineering* (EHB 2023), IFMBE Proceedings 111, Springer, 469-477 (2024). Knee, hip and ankle angles in Functional Movement Screen exercises of volleyball, basketball and soccer players. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-62523-7_52)
- Pavón Escobar FD, et al. Reliability of Kinovea as a low-cost tool for analysing biomechanical parameters in national volleyball athletes. Spiking and blocking in 13 pre-junior players of the Honduran national team. *Under review.*
