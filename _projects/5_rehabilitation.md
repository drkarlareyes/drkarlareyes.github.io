Clinical motion analysis is usually expensive. I evaluate open-source and camera-based tools against established methods, and design assistive devices that can be produced locally.

#### Publications

- Reyes Leiva KM, Zapata G, Heydari M, Villalba E, Imbert R. Design of an artificial vision system for exercise monitoring in healthy aging. *Journal of Biomimetics, Biomaterials and Biomedical Engineering* 71:129-138 (2026). Real-time posture and repetition feedback with MediaPipe and OpenCV for seven Vivifrail exercises; recognition from 91.06% to 100% in a pilot with 14 volunteers. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.4028/p-P2KnGL)
- Mejia Bronfield A, Reyes Leiva KM. Assessing the reliability of OpenCap and OpenSim as open source softwares for biomechanical analysis in neurological rehabilitation: a case study. *Journal of Biomimetics, Biomaterials and Biomedical Engineering* 66:37-46 (2024). Motricity Index tasks in a patient after stroke compared with three healthy participants. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.4028/p-M7j7bo)
- Zambrano S, Donaire R, Li S, Reyes K. Validation of Blaze-Pose as model in the analysis of lower extremities during gait. *IEEE E-Health and Bioengineering Conference* (2024). Hip, knee and ankle angles from pose estimation against manual analysis in Kinovea: hip and knee agreed, the ankle did not. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/EHB64556.2024.10805689)
- Alberto Serrano KD, Martínez Silva JD, Reyes Leiva KM. Finite element analysis on solid ankle-foot orthosis with additive manufacturing materials. *IEEE E-Health and Bioengineering Conference* (2024). Stress, deformation and stiffness of polypropylene, PLA, ABS and Nylon 101 orthoses of different thicknesses. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/EHB64556.2024.10805597)
