Inertial sensors make it possible to measure movement outside the laboratory. My work covers deep learning for activity recognition, the effect of segmentation choices such as window length, and gait and posture measurement for people with visual impairment.

#### Publications

- Jaén-Vargas M, Reyes-Leiva KM, Fernandes F, Serrano Olmedo JJ. Effects of sliding window variation in the performance of acceleration-based human activity recognition using deep learning models. *PeerJ Computer Science* 8:e1052 (2022). [Paper](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj-cs.1052)
- Reyes-Leiva KM, Cuba M, Serrano Olmedo JJ. Estimation of spatio-temporal parameters of gait and posture of visually impaired people using wearable sensors. *Sensors* (2023). [Paper](https://doi.org/10.3390/s23125564)

#### Code

- [deep-learning-in-time-series](https://github.com/drkarlareyes/deep-learning-in-time-series): DNN, CNN, LSTM and CNN-LSTM models on IMU and motion-capture data, including tests on people not seen in training.
