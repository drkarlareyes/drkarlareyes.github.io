## Industry projects

**Radar vital-sign monitoring, feasibility study** · 2026  
Assessed whether an impulse UWB radar can track breathing and heart rate in a real-world setting. I designed the signal processing and the evaluation, and recommended the next steps.

**Clinical decision support** · 2025–2026 · lead AI developer, consultant  
First stage of a model that supports a recurring prescribing decision from patients' visit records: data preparation, model design, and an evaluation on patients not seen in training.

**Digital twins for pharmaceutical processes** · 2024 · consultant  
Advised on the architecture of digital twin systems that link simulation models of pharmaceutical processes with incoming data streams.

**Secure automation pipelines** · 2023 · consultant  
Automation pipelines for fintech systems that handle U.S. tax reporting.
